What we check for you

Every household is different, so we only show what actually applies to yours.

FEMA

Individual Assistance for home repair, temporary housing, and essential needs.

SBA

Low-interest disaster loans — open to renters and homeowners, not just businesses.

State & local

Programs specific to your state or county that often go unnoticed.

Nonprofit help

Red Cross and community organizations — grants, not loans, no approval required first.

States we cover first

These five states have had the most federal disaster declarations, so we built their state programs first.

California

Wildfires drive its disaster declarations. 362 federal declarations since 1980, the most of any state.

Texas

355 federal declarations since 1980, from wildfires, hurricanes, floods and tornadoes.

Oklahoma

In Tornado Alley, with frequent severe storms and tornadoes.

Washington

Floods, storms and wildfires. Its own state aid program turns on for specific disasters.

Florida

Hurricanes: more federal hurricane declarations since 2003 than any other state.

Living in another state? You still get federal and nonprofit results, plus 211 for local programs.

Declaration counts: USAFacts and MoneyGeek analyses of FEMA data.

This is a navigation tool, not an application. We help you understand what you may qualify for and point you to the real FEMA, SBA, state, and nonprofit applications. We can't approve or deny anything.

Be careful of anyone asking for payment to "help" you apply for disaster aid. Legitimate assistance is always free.

One quick question before we start

Is anyone in your household, including a child, a U.S. citizen, a non-citizen national, or a qualified immigrant?

Some aid programs have citizenship rules, so your answer helps us show the right ones. It never stops you from using this tool.

Only one person in the household needs to qualify for FEMA individual aid. If the answer is no, you still have options such as Red Cross help. We never ask for names or documents, and nothing here is stored or sent.
